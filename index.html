Add it to your calendar

Every assignment, quiz and reminder becomes an all-day event on the day it is due, on its own separate calendar — one you can colour, hide or remove without touching your own. The evening to actually sit down and do it is noted inside each event. It refreshes by itself, and unlike this page it carries future weeks too, so next week's test shows up before next week.
